OCTAVE

Optical Coherence Tomography & Vibrometry Endoscope for Conductive Hearing Loss Diagnostics
NIH DEBUT Challenge 2nd Place Winner • UC Riverside Senior Design • Advisors: Dr. H. Park, Dr. W. Dong

View Project Details

Project Overview

The Problem

Conductive hearing loss (CHL) affects ~70 million people globally. Current diagnostics are fundamentally limited: otoscopy cannot visualize past the tympanic membrane; CT/MRI lack the spatial resolution (~0.5–0.625 mm) to resolve middle-ear structures and expose patients to ionizing radiation; exploratory surgery is invasive; and audiometry provides only subjective functional data without identifying which specific structure in the sound conduction pathway is compromised. No existing tool combines high-resolution structural imaging with quantitative functional vibrometry in a clinically practical form factor.

The Solution

OCTAVE is a handheld OCT and vibrometry endoscope for the external auditory meatus (EAM). It uses a 1310 nm swept-source OCT system (11 µm axial resolution, 2 mm imaging window) combined with Doppler vibrometry to simultaneously image middle-ear anatomy and quantify ossicular vibration in response to sound stimuli—all in a ~0.6 kg, 308 × 98 × 72 mm probe. The device integrates a Michelson interferometer with a MEMS mirror for lateral scanning, a compound linear stage for independent probe-depth and imaging-window adjustment, miniaturized SLA-printed optomechanical mounts, and a custom Windows software suite with real-time OCT/fiberscope visualization, photo/video capture, and vibrometry study controls.

Key Achievements

  • NIH DEBUT Challenge 2nd Place — nationally recognized undergraduate biomedical engineering innovation
  • 49.9% optical throughput after alignment (broadband 1310 nm SLD)
  • Sub-degree optomechanical precision — SLA-printed mounts with <1° warping, ±50 µm positional tolerance
  • Real-time clinical software — dual-window GUI (imaging + vibrometry) on 400×800 LCD + 1920×1080 monitor
  • Validated on human ear — tympanic membrane visualization, OCT fingertip imaging (3-layer skin structure resolved)
  • Patentable innovations — miniaturized optomechanics, integrated compound stage, alignment tools

My Role

Lead optomechanical designer on a 5-person team. Designed and manufactured the miniaturized optomechanical mounts (clamps, bases, silicone insulators) for all optical components (collimator, beam splitter, gold mirrors, MEMS mirror, doublet lenses, GRIN lenses). Engineered the compound two-tier linear stage system (main stage with rack-and-gear actuation + reference arm fine-adjustment stage). Designed the front assembly: otoscope head compatible with Welch Allyn disposable specula, integrated microphone/speaker unit for vibrometry, and GRIN lens protection channel. Performed ImageJ warping analysis to optimize SLA print parameters. Contributed to optical alignment verification (power meter measurements) and case integration.

Technical Details

Optical System

  • Laser Source: 1310 nm SLD (superluminescent diode), broadband
  • Configuration: Michelson interferometer (collimator → beam splitter → reference/sample arms)
  • Reference Arm: Gold-plated mirror on fine-adjustment stage (80 µm/rotation)
  • Sample Arm: MEMS mirror (2-axis scanning) → 2× achromatic doublets → GRIN lens (10 cm, 2.3 mm Ø)
  • Resolution: 11 µm axial, 2 mm imaging window
  • Throughput: 49.9% of input intensity after alignment

Mechanical Architecture

  • Probe Dimensions: 308 mm L × 98 mm W × 72 mm H (extended: 270 mm)
  • Weight: ~0.6 kg (probe only)
  • Compound Stage: Two-tier linear dovetail system
  • • Main stage: rack & gear actuation, 2–6 cm probe depth, manual brake
  • • Reference stage: fine-adjustment screw, 80 µm/turn, 5° max angular range
  • Optomechanical Mounts: SLA 3D printed (50 µm resolution), 3-part design (clamp + base + 3× silicone insulators)
  • Angular Adjustment: ~1.7°/turn per screw, 5° total per component
  • Case: Acrylic panels (2 mm & 4 mm), articulated arm mounting

Front Assembly & Patient Interface

  • Otoscope Head: Compatible with Welch Allyn disposable specula — protects patient (GRIN lens cannot contact EAM) and protects delicate GRIN lens
  • Fiberscope Integration: Co-axial with GRIN lens for direct EAM visualization during insertion/positioning
  • Speaker/Microphone Unit (SMU): Simultaneously induces TM/middle-ear vibration and measures sound pressure in EAM for vibrometry
  • Visible Alignment Laser: Superimposed on 1310 nm imaging laser for scan positioning (IR invisible to user)

Software Suite

Custom Windows GUI

  • Dual Display: 1920×1080 monitor + 400×800 touchscreen LCD
  • Patient Database: Input/submit patient data (name, DOB, gender); auto-creates per-patient folders for all OCT/vibrometry data
  • Imaging Window: Simultaneous OCT B-scan + live fiberscope feed; photo capture; video recording
  • Vibrometry Window: Real-time Doppler vibrometry analysis; "Conduct Study" button for automated measurement sequences
  • Navigation: Seamless switching between windows; logo-button exit on LCD
