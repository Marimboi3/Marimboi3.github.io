What This Is

A pair of fully interactive virtual cipher machines I built as an AR/VR Developer at UC Riverside's XCITE Center. Both run in VR and on desktop: you physically turn the rotors, set the plugboard, and watch the machine encrypt or decrypt a message in real time. The goal was to make historical cryptography tangible — something you operate, not just read about. I designed the 3D models and wrote the virtual encryption/decryption algorithms that drive both machines.

My Role

I led a team of three and owned the 3D modeling (the machines you can spin below) and the virtual cipher logic for both the Enigma and the arcade-style Cipher machine. We demoed it live to 60+ students and iterated on the interface from their usability feedback.

Why I'm Proud of It

It's the blend I love: accurate mechanical design plus working software. The models load right here on the page — drag to rotate, scroll to zoom — so you can inspect the real geometry, not just a screenshot.

Enigma Machine (1:1)

A faithful, life-size virtual replica of the WWII German Enigma. Set the rotors and plugboard and it encodes/decodes messages using the real historical stepping and wiring logic.

Drag to rotate • scroll or pinch to zoom • lid and front flap open so you can see the lampboard, keyboard, and plugboard
