About Me

I am a computational biomedical engineer focused on developing interactive and human-centered technologies across embedded systems, wearable ciomedical devices, XR development, and rapid prototyping. My work combines hardware, software, and design to create systems that bridge engineering and human interaction. Across research and development projects, I have worked on wearable neural imaging devices, augmented and virtual reality learning environments, biomedical instrumentation, embedded firmware, and physiological signal processing. I am particularly interested in building technologies that make complex systems more intuitive, immersive, and accessible - especially in areas such as biomedical devices, educational technology, and interactive computing.
