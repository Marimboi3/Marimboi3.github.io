About This Work

These are designs I built in Fusion 360 outside of the larger case-study projects. They range from optical holders and lab hardware to personal and commissioned prints. Each is modeled to be manufacturable — most were printed and iterated on in person. Where a render is part of a bigger project (like the VR cipher machines or the OCTAVE probe), it lives on that project's page instead.
