What This Is

A pair of fully interactive, virtual cipher machines I built for an undergraduate XR job. Both run in VR and on desktop: you physically turn the rotors, set the plugboard, and watch the machine encrypt or decrypt a message in real time. The goal was to make historical cryptography tangible — something you operate, not just read about. I designed the 3D models and wrote the virtual encryption/decryption algorithms that drive both machines.

My Role

I owned the 3D modeling (the machines you can spin below) and the virtual cipher logic for both the Enigma and the arcade-style Cipher machine. The Unity project was a team effort; my contributions were the geometry and the encryption/decryption behavior.

Why I'm Proud of It

It's the blend I love: accurate mechanical design plus working software. The models load right here on the page — drag to rotate, scroll to zoom — so you can inspect the real geometry, not just a screenshot.
